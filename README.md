## ⚡ Professional Interests

### 🌐 Grid Infrastructure & Power Systems
Interested in the design and protection of high-voltage transmission and distribution networks. My focus includes substation layouts, protection coordination (relays and sensing), and the integration of renewable energy sources into the existing grid.

### ⚙️ Electrical Machines & Drives
Passionate about the physics and control of rotating machinery. My work includes the study of motor construction, winding configurations, and the numerical modeling of transient characteristics in DC and AC machines.

### 🏢 Building Services & Integrated Systems
Exploring the internal power distribution of commercial and industrial facilities. This includes load calculations, circuit protection, and optimizing electrical systems for efficiency and safety within the built environment.
