LEXIN: breadboard to PCB

KiCad schematic and fabrication-ready PCB for LEXIN, plus a report on every engineering change made along the way.

The problem

The breadboard proved the wake-word and cloud flow, but a manufacturable board meant revisiting power, parts and protocols.

Worth knowing

  • Design evolution report records the circuit that was built, not only the first prototype.
  • ERC and DRC reports are included so the checks are visible.
  • The same schematic and board files open directly in KiCad.

How it works

  • Formalised the circuit as a complete KiCad schematic and PCB layout.
  • Each change was driven by a concrete constraint: component availability, power budget, protocol reliability or manufacturability.
  • The breadboard’s boost converter limited amplifier power, and the report documents how the final board handles power instead.
  • Each change in the report compares the breadboard approach with the final PCB implementation.
  • Comparing the schematics: the TPA3116D2 amplifier module became two TDA2030 amplifier channels, and the microphone moved to an INMP441 connector.
