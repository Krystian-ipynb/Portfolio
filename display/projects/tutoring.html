First Principles Tutoring automation

Excel and VBA tools that handle the admin side of a small tutoring business: records, finances and SMS messages.

  • VBA
  • MacroDroid
  • Small business
FocusAutomation
ToolsExcel, VBA
MessagingMacroDroid webhook
StatusIn use

The problem

Running a tutoring business means repeating the same admin every week. These tools take over the repetitive parts.

Worth knowing

  • Payment details and the webhook address sit as constants at the top of the module, so setup is one edit.
  • The business has its own public website.

How it works

  • An Excel workbook with a VBA automation module (now at v3) runs the day-to-day tasks.
  • The module sends SMS messages by calling a MacroDroid webhook from an Android phone.
  • A separate finance workbook handles basic accounting for the business.
